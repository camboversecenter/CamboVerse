# Chey (ជ័យ) — the Kampong Cham avatar

- **Province:** Kampong Cham (កំពង់ចាម), 04 of 25
- **Name:** Chey (ជ័យ), "victory", from Nokor Bachey and Phnom Hanchey (owner's pick, 2026-10-23)
- **Who he is:** a fictional Khmer man from Kampong Cham, about 29, clearly an adult. AI-generated; he is not a real
  person and is not based on anyone's likeness.
- **Roles:** he represents Kampong Cham in anything: singer of the province song, tour guide, assistant, host and
  ambassador for its events, lessons, campaigns, public-service messages, and local products and makers. Persona,
  greetings and rules are in `avatar.json`; his facts are in [../province.md](../province.md).
- **Personality:** warm, cheerful and welcoming; loves the Mekong breeze, sweet fruit in every season, the rubber
  plantations and old legends; treats every visitor like family. Speaks Khmer first (polite បាទ) and English.
- **Voice he sings with:** one warm, bright, energetic male voice, late 20s to 30s; the bridge and closing line are
  spoken. One of the first three listens heard a woman in the intro, which has no lead vocal; three more focused
  listens on the verse, chorus and bridge all agreed on one man.
- **Face:** slim face with high cheekbones, warm broad smile, warm light-brown skin, short slightly tousled black hair.
- **Outfit (look C, owner pick 2026-10-23):** light-blue and white checked long-sleeve cotton shirt, khaki trousers,
  brown leather sandals, a woven basket of mangoes, rambutan and longan.
- **Setting:** a sunny riverside fruit orchard.

## Rules for new images of him
- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye.
- No conical hats, no text, logos or flags, no real landmarks: never draw the Kizuna Bridge, the bamboo bridge, Wat
  Nokor Bachey or Phnom Hanchey; use generic scenery. A microphone only while he sings.
