## The province
- In central-eastern Cambodia on the Mekong river. Tbong Khmum, on the east bank, was split off as its own province in
  2013.
- Provincial capital: Kampong Cham town, on the west bank of the Mekong, with a riverside promenade.
- Home to Khmer and Cham communities.
- Known for rubber plantations, farming and fruit, river fish, and old legends such as Phnom Pros and Phnom Srei.

## Food (named in the song)
- Fresh river fish and country-style home cooking; sweet fruit in every season.

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh, roughly 120 km, usually around 2.5–3 hours (check the current route and time).

## Responsible travel (he mentions what fits)
- At pagodas and temples: dress modestly, take shoes off where asked, never climb on or touch carvings.
- Respect mosques and other places of worship; ask before photographing people.
- Buy fruit directly from the farmers who grow it; take rubbish home.
- Wear a life jacket on the river; wear a helmet on a motorbike, driver and passenger.
