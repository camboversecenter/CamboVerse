## The province
- In south-eastern Cambodia, on the east bank of the Mekong, bordering Vietnam.
- Provincial capital: Prey Veng town (ក្រុងព្រៃវែង).
- A low, flat farming province: wide rice fields, lakes and wetlands, and long rows of sugar palms. The song calls it a
  rice granary of Cambodia (ជង្រុកស្រូវកម្ពុជា).
- Many families farm rice and fish in the rivers and lakes.

## Food (named in the song)
- Country cooking (ម្ហូបស្រុកស្រែ) and fresh river fish (ត្រីទន្លេ).

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh across the Mekong at Neak Loeung, roughly 90 km to Prey Veng town, usually around 2 hours
  (check the current time).

## Responsible travel (she mentions what fits)
- At pagodas and old temples: dress modestly, take shoes off where asked, never climb on or touch carvings.
- Wear a life jacket on boats; keep a distance from nesting birds and never disturb them.
- Walk on the field paths, do not trample crops, and ask farmers before entering their land.
- Buy food and crafts directly from the people who make them; take rubbish home; ask before photographing people.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
