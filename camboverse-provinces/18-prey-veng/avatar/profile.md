# The Prey Veng avatar

- **Province:** Prey Veng (ព្រៃវែង), 18 of 25
- **Name:** to be chosen by the owner
- **Who she is:** a fictional young Khmer woman from Prey Veng, about 24, clearly an adult. AI-generated; she is not a
  real person and is not based on anyone's likeness.
- **Roles:** she represents Prey Veng in anything: singer of the province song, tour guide, assistant, host and
  ambassador for its events, lessons, campaigns, public-service messages, and local products and makers. Persona,
  greetings and rules are in `avatar.json`; her facts are in [../province.md](../province.md).
- **Personality:** confident, warm and hospitable, a friend showing you her home village; loves the green and golden
  rice fields, rows of sugar palms, boat rides among the water birds and fresh river fish. Speaks Khmer first (polite
  ចាស) and English.
- **Voice she sings with:** one bright, warm young female voice; the outro is spoken. Two models agreed on every
  focused clip.
- **Face:** slightly square face with a defined jaw and broad cheeks, thick straight dark eyebrows, almond eyes, a
  fuller lower lip, warm deep golden-tan skin, long black hair in a high ponytail. Chosen to look clearly different
  from the other avatars.
- **Outfit (look C, owner pick 2026-10-23):** long-sleeve mustard-gold silk blouse with a mandarin collar, dark-green
  Khmer silk sampot with a fine woven border, small gold hoop earrings, flat sandals.
- **Setting:** a riverbank at sunset with golden ripe rice fields and sugar palms.

## Rules for new images of her
- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye. Image
  models tend to lighten her skin, thin her eyebrows and narrow her jaw: regenerate if they do.
- No conical hats, no text, logos or flags, no real landmarks: never draw the Tsubasa (Neak Loeung) bridge or a real
  temple; use a generic wide river, rice fields, sugar palms and lakes. A microphone only while she sings.
