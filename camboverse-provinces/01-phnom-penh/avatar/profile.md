# Penh (ពេញ) — the Phnom Penh avatar

- **Province:** Phnom Penh (ភ្នំពេញ), the capital, 01 of 25
- **Name:** Penh (ពេញ), after Daun Penh, the woman whose legend gave the city its name (owner's pick, 2026-10-23)
- **Who she is:** a fictional young Khmer woman from Phnom Penh, about 24, clearly an adult. AI-generated; she is not
  a real person and is not based on anyone's likeness.
- **Roles:** she represents Phnom Penh in anything: singer of the city song, tour guide, assistant, host and
  ambassador for its events, lessons, campaigns, public-service messages, and local products and makers. Persona,
  greetings and rules are in `avatar.json`; her facts are in [../province.md](../province.md).
- **Personality:** sweet, calm and creative, a young artist; loves slow riverside walks at sunset, coffee by the
  water, night markets, street food and the city's young creators. Speaks Khmer first (polite ចាស) and English.
- **Voice she sings with:** one sweet, soft, smooth young female voice; the bridge and outro are spoken. Three focused
  listens agreed.
- **Face:** gentle face with soft full cheeks and a softly pointed chin, strong dark eyebrows, medium-brown skin,
  centre-parted black hair in two low braids, several small silver earrings.
- **Outfit (look D, owner pick 2026-10-23):** loose white linen shirt with small paint smudges, high-waisted olive
  trousers, canvas shoes, a paintbrush.
- **Setting:** a bright modern art studio with colourful abstract paintings.

## Rules for new images of her
- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye.
- **Never draw Phnom Penh's landmarks** (Royal Palace, Wat Phnom, Central Market, Independence Monument, skyline,
  bridges): use generic riverside, street, café and market scenes. No conical hats, no text, logos or flags. A
  microphone only while she sings.
