## The city
- Cambodia's capital and largest city, at the meeting of the Mekong, Tonle Sap and Bassac rivers, a stretch called
  Chaktomuk (ចតុមុខ, "four faces").
- Once nicknamed the "Pearl of Asia" (គុជអាស៊ី) for its riverside boulevards and buildings.
- Its name comes from the legend of Daun Penh, who founded a shrine on a small hill, Wat Phnom.
- A young, fast-growing city with a busy café culture, night markets, street food, and a creative arts scene.

## Places (named in the song)
| Place | What to know |
|---|---|
| Royal Palace (ព្រះបរមរាជវាំង) | the King's residence by the river, with the Silver Pagoda; dress modestly to visit |
| Wat Phnom (វត្តភ្នំ) | hilltop pagoda at the heart of the city's founding legend |
| Riverside (មាត់ទន្លេ) | the promenade along the river, best at sunrise and sunset, with boats on the water |
| Central Market (ផ្សារធំថ្មី) | a domed market hall from the 1930s, full of stalls |
| Night markets (ផ្សាររាត្រី) | evening food and shopping |

Places of memory, not in the song but often asked about: the Tuol Sleng Genocide Museum and the Choeung Ek memorial.
Visit them quietly and with respect.

## Getting around
- Tuk-tuks, ride-hailing apps, buses and motorbike taxis; boat trips on the river. Check the current international
  airport and transport options before travelling.

## Responsible travel (she mentions what fits)
- Dress modestly at the Royal Palace, pagodas and temples; take shoes off where asked.
- Be quiet and respectful at genocide memorial sites.
- Never give money or sweets to children selling or begging; support schools and community projects instead.
- Cross busy streets carefully; wear a helmet on a motorbike, driver and passenger.
- Support local artists, makers and street-food sellers; take rubbish home; ask before photographing people.
