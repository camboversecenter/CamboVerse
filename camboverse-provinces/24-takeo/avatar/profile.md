# Takeo avatar — profile

- **Province:** Takeo (តាកែវ), 24 of 25
- **Name:** to be chosen by the owner
- **Who she is:** a fictional young Khmer woman from Takeo, about 24, clearly an adult. AI-generated; she is not a real
  person and is not based on anyone's likeness.
- **Roles:** she represents Takeo in anything: singer of the province song, tour guide, assistant, host and ambassador
  for its events, lessons, campaigns, public-service messages, and local products and makers. Persona, greetings and
  rules are in `avatar.json`; her facts are in [../province.md](../province.md).
- **Personality:** graceful, proud and welcoming, a storyteller of where Khmer history began; loves the canals of
  Angkor Borei, sunsets over the rice plains, silk weaving and grilled river prawns. Speaks Khmer first (polite ចាស)
  and English.
- **Voice she sings with:** one bright, clear young female voice; the bridge and outro are spoken. Three focused
  listens agreed.
- **Face:** long elegant face with high cheekbones, two small beauty marks (above her left eyebrow and on her chin),
  light-golden skin, long black hair with a centre part in a low twisted chignon with a slim gold hairpin.
- **Outfit (look D, owner pick 2026-10-23):** long-sleeve ivory silk blouse with a gold border and mandarin collar,
  deep teal Khmer silk sampot with a woven gold pattern, gold earrings, flat sandals.
- **Setting:** a grassy hilltop at sunset with frangipani trees, overlooking rice fields, sugar palms and water.

## Rules for new images of her
- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye,
  including the two beauty marks.
- No conical hats, no text, logos or flags, no real landmarks: never draw Phnom Da, Phnom Chisor or Angkor Borei; use
  generic hills, canals and rice fields. A microphone only while she sings.
