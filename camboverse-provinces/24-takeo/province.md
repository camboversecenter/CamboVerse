## The province
- In southern Cambodia, south of Phnom Penh, bordering Vietnam.
- Provincial capital: Doun Kaev (ក្រុងដូនកែវ), often called Takeo town.
- Called a **cradle of Khmer civilisation**: the area of Angkor Borei and Phnom Da was an early centre of the Funan
  period (ហ្វូណន), long before Angkor.
- Landscape: rice plains, canals and wetlands that flood in the rainy season, and small hills rising from the plains.
- Known for silk weaving, including hol (ហូល) and pidan (ពិតាន), and for river prawns.

## Places (named in the song)
| Place | What to know |
|---|---|
| Angkor Borei (អង្គរបុរី) | ancient town of the Funan period with old canals; a small museum of early Khmer art |
| Phnom Da (ប្រាសាទភ្នំដា) | ancient temple on a hill near Angkor Borei, reached by boat across the flooded fields in the rainy season |
| Phnom Chisor (ភ្នំជីសូរ) | hilltop temple reached by a long stairway, with wide views over the rice plains |
| Silk villages | hol and pidan weaving on wooden hand looms |

## Food (named in the song)
- **Grilled river prawns (បង្គាដុត)**, the province's best-known dish.

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh, roughly 80 km south, usually around 1.5–2 hours (check the current time).

## Responsible travel (she mentions what fits)
- At pagodas and ancient temples: dress modestly, take shoes off where asked, never climb on or touch carvings.
- Wear a life jacket on the boats to Phnom Da; take care on the long stairways up the hills.
- Buy silk and food directly from the people who make them; take rubbish home; ask before photographing people.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
