## The province
- In southern-central Cambodia, almost completely surrounding the capital, Phnom Penh, and bordering Vietnam.
- Provincial capital: Ta Khmau (ក្រុងតាខ្មៅ), just south of Phnom Penh.
- A province of rivers: the Mekong, the Tonle Sap and the Bassac (ទន្លេបាសាក់) flow through it, with river islands,
  orchards, rice fields and sugar palms.
- Known for **silk weaving** on hand looms in its river-island villages.

## Places (named in the song)
| Place | What to know |
|---|---|
| Oudong (ឧដុង្គ), Phnom Preah Reach Trop (ភ្នំព្រះរាជទ្រព្យ) | royal capital of Cambodia for more than two centuries (about 1618–1866); a hill with stupas and long stairways and wide views over the countryside; a sacred place (do not draw it in images) |
| Koh Oknha Tey (កោះឧកញ៉ាតី) | a Mekong island known for silk weaving, reached by ferry |
| Koh Dach (កោះដាច់) | the well-known silk island named in the chorus; **it is now part of Phnom Penh** (Chroy Changvar), next to Kandal |
| Riverside villages | sugar palms, old pagodas and sunsets over the rivers |

## Food (named in the song)
- Country cooking (ម្ហូបស្រុកស្រែ) and fresh river fish (ត្រីទន្លេ).

## Getting there
- Kandal begins at the edge of Phnom Penh. Oudong is roughly 40 km north of the city, usually around 1 hour by road
  (check the current time). The silk islands are reached by small ferries.

## Responsible travel (she mentions what fits)
- At Oudong, pagodas and stupas: dress modestly, take shoes off where asked, be quiet and respectful; take care on the
  long stairways.
- Wear a life jacket on boats and ferries.
- Buy silk directly from the weavers, and ask before photographing them at work.
- Take rubbish home; ask before photographing people.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
