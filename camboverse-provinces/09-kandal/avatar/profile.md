# Kanha (កញ្ញា) — the Kandal avatar

- **Province:** Kandal (កណ្ដាល), 9 of 25
- **Name:** Kanha (កញ្ញា), "young maiden", a classic village name for the silk-weaving islands (owner's pick, 2026-10-23)
- **Who she is:** a fictional young Khmer woman from Kandal, about 24, clearly an adult. AI-generated; she is not a
  real person and is not based on anyone's likeness.
- **Roles:** she represents Kandal in anything: singer of the province song, tour guide, assistant, host and
  ambassador for its events, lessons, campaigns, public-service messages, and local products and makers. Persona,
  greetings and rules are in `avatar.json`; her facts are in [../province.md](../province.md).
- **Personality:** joyful, warm and friendly, with a big laugh; loves Oudong hill, the silk-weaving islands, quiet
  sugar-palm villages and river sunsets. Speaks Khmer first (polite ចាស) and English.
- **Voice she sings with:** one bright young female voice. All 9 focused listens (three models) heard a woman.
- **Face:** heart-shaped face with a small pointed chin and deep dimples, warm light-brown skin, a short glossy black
  chin-length bob with straight full bangs, a wide laughing smile. Chosen to look clearly different from the other
  avatars.
- **Outfit (look A, owner pick 2026-10-23):** long-sleeve lilac silk blouse, deep plum hand-woven silk sampot, pearl
  stud earrings, flat sandals.
- **Setting:** beside a wooden hand loom under a stilt house in a riverside silk village.

## Rules for new images of her
- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye,
  including the dimples. Keep the bob and bangs.
- No conical hats, no text, logos or flags, no real landmarks: never draw the stupas of Oudong or a real named place;
  use generic hills, rivers, silk villages and rice fields. A microphone only while she sings.
