## The province
- In eastern Cambodia, east of the Mekong, bordering Vietnam. It became a province in 2013, formed from the eastern
  part of Kampong Cham.
- Provincial capital: Suong (ក្រុងសួង).
- Known for its **red soil** (ដីក្រហម) and **rubber plantations** (ចម្ការកៅស៊ូ), and for growing fruit, cashew nuts
  and other crops; the song calls it a land of agro-industry (កសិឧស្សាហកម្ម).

| Suong market (ផ្សារសួង) | the busy market of the provincial capital, with food and fresh fruit |

## Food (named in the song)
- Fresh fruit of the season (ផ្លែឈើស្រស់ៗ) and the many dishes of Suong market.

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh via Kampong Cham and across the Mekong, roughly 160 km to Suong, usually around 3 hours
  (check the current time).

## Responsible travel (he mentions what fits)
- Plantations and orchards are private farms: enter only with the owner's permission; never pick fruit or tap trees
  without asking.
- At old sites: never climb on walls or take stones or pottery.
- At pagodas: dress modestly, take shoes off where asked.
- Buy fruit and food directly from local sellers; take rubbish home; ask before photographing people.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
