## The song
A warm welcome to the red-earth province of the east: green rubber plantations, rich soil and fresh fruit, the old
walls of Banteay Prei Nokor, the story of Sdach Korn, and the lively market of Suong. Listen:
[song/tbong-khmum-land-of-plenty.mp3](./song/tbong-khmum-land-of-plenty.mp3). Lyrics: [song/lyrics.txt](./song/lyrics.txt).
