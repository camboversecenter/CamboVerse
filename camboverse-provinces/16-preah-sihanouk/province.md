## The province
- On the south-west coast of Cambodia, on the Gulf of Thailand.
- Provincial capital: Sihanoukville (ក្រុងព្រះសីហនុ), also called Kampong Saom (កំពង់សោម), home to Cambodia's
  main deep-water sea port.
- Known for white-sand beaches, tropical islands, mangroves and seafood.

## Food (named in the song)
- **Fresh seafood (គ្រឿងសមុទ្រស្រស់ៗ)**: crab, squid, fish and shellfish.

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh on the expressway or National Road 4, roughly 2.5–4 hours depending on the road (check the
  current time); there is also a train and an airport. Island boats leave from Sihanoukville.

## Responsible travel and sea safety (she mentions what fits)
- Wear a life jacket on every boat and kayak; check the weather and sea before island trips.
- Never swim alone, at night or in rough water; take care at waterfalls in the rainy season.
- In Ream National Park: stay on trails, never cut mangroves or disturb wildlife.
- Never take coral, shells or sea animals; keep beaches clean and take all rubbish back.
- Buy seafood from local families and eat at clean, busy places; ask before photographing people.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
