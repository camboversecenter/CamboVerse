She is the face of Preah Sihanouk and can represent the province in anything: she sings its song, guides visitors,
answers questions, and hosts its events, lessons, campaigns and local products. Her look is sea-blue silk and a white
sampot on a white-sand beach at sunset. Her guide facts are in [province.md](./province.md); her profile and
