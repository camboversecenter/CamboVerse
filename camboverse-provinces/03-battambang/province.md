## The province
- In north-west Cambodia, bordering Thailand to the west. Known as **Cambodia's rice bowl** for its vast rice fields.
- Provincial capital: Battambang city (ក្រុងបាត់ដំបង), on the Stung Sangke river (ស្ទឹងសង្កែ), one of Cambodia's
  largest cities, with an old town of French colonial-era shophouses.
- Name: from the legend of **Ta Dambang Kranhoung** (លោកតាដំបងក្រញូង), "the lost stick".
- Known as a home of the arts: painters, musicians, and the arts school and circus of Phare Ponleu Selpak
  (ផារ៍ពន្លឺសិល្បៈ).

## Places (named in the song)
| Place | What to know |
|---|---|
| Old town | colonial-era shophouses and streets by the Sangke river, good for walking and photos |
| Bamboo train (ឡូរី / norry) | small bamboo platforms that run on rails through the rice fields |
| Phnom Banan (ភ្នំបាណន់) | an ancient temple on a hilltop reached by a long stone stairway, with views over the rice fields |
| Phnom Sampov (ភ្នំសំពៅ) | hill with caves; at dusk millions of bats fly out of a cave in long streams |
| Wat Samrong Knong (វត្តសំរោងក្នុង) | old pagoda with a history to learn about (treat it as a place of memory) |
| Ek Phnom (ប្រាសាទឯកភ្នំ) | ancient temple ruins |
| Wat Kor village (វត្តគរ) | traditional old wooden houses |
| Kamping Puoy (ទំនប់កំពីងពួយ) | reservoir with lotus and boat rides |
| Stung Sangke (ស្ទឹងសង្កែ) | boat rides and sunsets on the river |

## Food (named in the song)
- **Mee kola (មីកុឡា)**, local sausage, **nem** (ណែមត្រី), night markets by the river.
- Sweet local **oranges** (ក្រូចពោធិ៍សាត់បាត់ដំបង).

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh on National Road 5, roughly 290 km, usually around 5–6 hours (check the current time).

## Responsible travel (she mentions what fits)
- At pagodas and temples: dress modestly, take shoes off where asked, never climb on or touch carvings.
- Treat memorial sites with quiet respect.
- Buy art, crafts and food directly from the people who make them.
- Take care on the bamboo train and on boats; take rubbish home; ask before photographing people.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
