# Sophea (សោភា) — the Svay Rieng avatar

- **Province:** Svay Rieng (ស្វាយរៀង), 23 of 25
- **Name:** Sophea (សោភា), "graceful beauty", elegant like her look (owner's pick, 2026-10-24)
- **Who she is:** a fictional young Khmer woman from Svay Rieng, about 24, clearly an adult. AI-generated; she is not a
  real person and is not based on anyone's likeness.
- **Roles:** she represents Svay Rieng in anything: singer of the province song, tour guide, assistant, host and
  ambassador for its events, lessons, campaigns, public-service messages, and local products and makers. Persona,
  greetings and rules are in `avatar.json`; her facts are in [../province.md](../province.md).
- **Personality:** warm, confident and elegant; loves the Waiko riverside, rice fields and sugar palms, the temple of
  Basak, river sunsets and banh kanh. Speaks Khmer first (polite ចាស) and English.
- **Voice she sings with:** one bright young female voice. All 9 focused listens (three models) heard a woman.
- **Face:** long oval face with a defined jaw, thick dark arched eyebrows, two small beauty marks (beside the right side
  of her nose and on the left side of her chin), warm tan skin, shoulder-length softly wavy black hair with a deep side
  part. Chosen to look clearly different from the other avatars.
- **Outfit (look C, owner pick 2026-10-24):** long-sleeve emerald-green silk blouse with a gold border, dark-green-and-
  gold Khmer silk sampot, small gold hoop earrings, flat sandals.
- **Setting:** an earth path through green rice fields between rows of sugar palms in late-afternoon light.

## Rules for new images of her
- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye,
  including the two beauty marks.
- No conical hats, no text, logos or flags, no real landmarks: never draw Prasat Basak or the Bavet border gate; use
  generic rivers, gardens, rice fields and sugar palms. A microphone only while she sings.
