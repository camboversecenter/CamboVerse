## The province
- In south-eastern Cambodia, east of the Mekong, with Vietnam on three sides.
- Provincial capital: Svay Rieng town (ក្រុងស្វាយរៀង), on the Waiko river (ទន្លេវ៉ៃគោ), with riverside gardens.
- Flat green rice country with long rows of sugar palms.
- Bavet (ក្រុងបាវិត) is a busy town at the main road border crossing to Vietnam (ច្រកទ្វារអន្តរជាតិ).

## Food (named in the song)
- **Banh kanh (បាញ់កាញ់)**, a thick noodle soup well loved in Svay Rieng.

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh on National Road 1 across the Mekong, roughly 125 km, usually around 2.5–3 hours (check the
  current time).

## Responsible travel (she mentions what fits)
- At old temples: never climb on stones or take anything. At pagodas: dress modestly, take shoes off where asked.
- At the border: follow official rules and carry the right documents.
- Eat at clean, busy stalls and buy from local families; take rubbish home; ask before photographing people.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
