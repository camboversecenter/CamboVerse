## The province
- A small province in far western Cambodia, in the hills next to Battambang, bordering Thailand.
- Provincial capital: Pailin town (ក្រុងប៉ៃលិន).
- Known for **gems**, above all rubies (ត្បូងទទឹម) and sapphires (ត្បូងកណ្ដៀង), and for gem cutting and jewellery;
  the song calls it ទឹកដីត្បូងពេជ្រ, "land of gems".
- Cooler hills and forests, with orchards of longan (មៀន) and other fruit.

## Places (named in the song)
| Place | What to know |
|---|---|
| Phnom Yat (ភ្នំយ៉ាត) and its pagoda | a hill with a pagoda and wide views over the town; linked to the legend of Yeay Yat (យាយយ៉ាត); a place of worship (do not draw it in images) |
| Ou Tavao waterfall (ទឹកធ្លាក់អូរតាវ៉ៅ) | a forest waterfall where people swim and picnic; **location and access to verify** |
| Gem shops and workshops | where local craftsmen cut and set stones |

## Food (named in the song)
- **Pailin longans (មៀនប៉ៃលិន)**, sweet and well known, and other fresh fruit.

## Getting there
- By road from Battambang, roughly 80 km west, usually around 1.5–2 hours; from Phnom Penh via Battambang (check the
  current time).

## Responsible travel and safety (he mentions what fits)
- **Landmine safety:** stay on roads and well-used paths; never walk into fields, forest or old ground without a local
  guide, because landmines and unexploded ordnance may remain in rural areas; never touch suspicious objects and report
  them to the police or local authorities.
- Buy gems only from reputable shops and ask for a receipt; be careful of claims about value.
- Take care at waterfalls, especially in the rainy season.
- At pagodas: dress modestly, take shoes off where asked.
- Take rubbish home; ask before photographing people; wear a helmet on a motorbike.
