## The province
- In western Cambodia, between the Tonle Sap lake in the east and the Cardamom Mountains (ជួរភ្នំក្រវាញ) in the west,
  bordering Thailand.
- Provincial capital: Pursat town (ក្រុងពោធិ៍សាត់), on the Pursat river.
- The Cardamom Mountains hold some of the largest rainforests in mainland South-East Asia, with protected areas.
- Known for **Pursat oranges** (ក្រូចពោធិ៍សាត់) and for **marble carving** (ចម្លាក់ថ្មកែវ).

## Food (named in the song)
- **Pursat oranges (ក្រូចពោធិ៍សាត់)**, sweet and well known.

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh on National Road 5, roughly 185 km, usually around 3–4 hours (check the current time).

## Responsible travel (he mentions what fits)
- In the mountains: go with local guides or rangers, stay on trails, never hunt, buy wildlife or cut trees.
- Take care at waterfalls, especially in the rainy season; wear a life jacket on boats.
- At the floating village: respect people's homes and privacy, ask before photographing, and buy from local families.
- At shrines and pagodas: dress modestly, take shoes off where asked.
- Buy oranges and crafts directly from the people who grow and make them; take rubbish home.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
