# The Pursat avatar

- **Province:** Pursat (ពោធិ៍សាត់), 19 of 25
- **Name:** to be chosen by the owner
- **Who he is:** a fictional young Khmer man from Pursat, about 26, clearly an adult. AI-generated; he is not a real
  person and is not based on anyone's likeness.
- **Roles:** he represents Pursat in anything: singer of the province song, tour guide, assistant, host and ambassador
  for its events, lessons, campaigns, public-service messages, and local products and makers. Persona, greetings and
  rules are in `avatar.json`; his facts are in [../province.md](../province.md).
- **Personality:** cheerful, energetic and outdoorsy; loves the misty Cardamom ridges, forest waterfalls, the floating
  village, marble carving and sweet Pursat oranges. Speaks Khmer first (polite បាទ) and English.
- **Voice he sings with:** one bright young male voice (high tenor). The models disagreed on gender; the owner
  confirmed a man.
- **Face:** square face with a strong jaw and a small cleft chin, clean-shaven, light-golden skin, thick black hair in
  a short brushed-up quiff with tapered sides, a big open smile. Chosen to look clearly different from the other
  avatars (unlike Seila, no goatee and a square, not long, face).
- **Outfit (look B, owner pick 2026-10-23):** white polo shirt, orange-and-cream checked krama over one shoulder,
  navy chinos, white sneakers.
- **Setting:** a sunny citrus orchard with green hills behind.

## Rules for new images of him
- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye,
  including the cleft chin.
- No conical hats, no text, logos or flags, no real landmarks: never draw the Oknha Khleang Moeung shrine or a real
  named place; use generic mountains, waterfalls, orchards and lake villages. A microphone only while he sings.
