## The province
- In central Cambodia, north of Phnom Penh on National Road 5, along the Tonle Sap river.
- Provincial capital: Kampong Chhnang town.
- The name means "port of pots": the province is famous for **pottery** (កុលាលភាជន៍), with whole villages making
  clay pots and water jars by hand and firing them in wood kilns, a skill passed down for generations.
- Landscape: rice fields, sugar palms, the Tonle Sap river and its floating villages, and Phnom Neang Kangrei.

## Places (named in the song)
| Place | What to know |
|---|---|
| Pottery villages (ភូមិកុលាលភាជន៍) | families shaping clay pots by hand; visitors can try making one |
| Floating villages (ភូមិបណ្ដែតទឹក) | homes and boats on the water, best seen by boat |
| Phnom Neang Kangrei (ភ្នំនាងកង្រី) | a hill known for its old legend, with views over the countryside |

## Food (named in the song)
- Country cooking and fresh river fish.

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh on National Road 5, roughly 90 km, usually around 2 hours (check the current time).

## Responsible travel (he mentions what fits)
- Buy pottery directly from the families who make it; ask before photographing them at work.
- Wear a life jacket on boats to the floating villages, and respect the families who live there.
- Take care on hill paths; take rubbish home. Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
