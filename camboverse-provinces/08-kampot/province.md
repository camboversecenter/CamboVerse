## The province
- On Cambodia's south coast by the Gulf of Thailand, next to Kep.
- Provincial capital: Kampot town, on the Prek Kampot / Prek Tuek Chhu river (ព្រែកកំពត), known for its calm riverside
  and sunsets.
- Famous for **Kampot pepper** (ម្រេចកំពត), a protected geographical indication, and for sea salt from the salt
  fields near the coast.
- Landscape: river, mangroves, salt fields, pepper and fruit farms, and the cool, misty Bokor mountain
  (ភ្នំបូកគោ) in Preah Monivong Bokor National Park.

## Food (named in the song)
- Fresh seafood stir-fried with Kampot pepper; nom banh chok with pepper; Kampot mee kiev.
- Fruit: Kampot durian, Tuek Chhou pineapple, coconuts, mangosteen and sapodilla (សាវម៉ាវ).

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh on National Road 3, roughly 150 km, usually around 3–4 hours (check the current time).

## Responsible travel (he mentions what fits)
- Buy pepper, salt and fruit directly from the farmers who grow them.
- Wear a life jacket on river boats; do not disturb the fireflies or mangroves.
- Drive the mountain road slowly; the weather on Bokor can change fast.
- At pagodas: dress modestly and take shoes off where asked. Take rubbish home; ask before photographing people.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
