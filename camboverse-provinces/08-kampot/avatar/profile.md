# Theara (ធារា) — the Kampot avatar

- **Province:** Kampot (កំពត), 08 of 25
- **Name:** Theara (ធារា), "flowing water", for the Prek Kampot river and Tuek Chhou (owner's pick, 2026-10-23)
- **Who he is:** a fictional young Khmer man from Kampot, about 28, clearly an adult. AI-generated; he is not a real
  person and is not based on anyone's likeness.
- **Roles:** he represents Kampot in anything: singer of the province song, tour guide, assistant, host and
  ambassador for its events, lessons, campaigns, public-service messages, and local products and makers. Persona,
  greetings and rules are in `avatar.json`; his facts are in [../province.md](../province.md).
- **Personality:** calm, warm and gentle; grew up among pepper vines; loves the river at sunset, the cool mountain
  air, the fireflies in the mangroves and slow, relaxing travel. Speaks Khmer first (polite បាទ) and English.
- **Voice he sings with:** one warm, smooth, mellow male voice, sung (no rap), mid-20s to mid-30s. Three focused
  listens agreed.
- **Face:** kind face with a broad jaw, gentle smile, warm light-brown skin, short neat black hair.
- **Outfit (look A, owner pick 2026-10-23):** olive-green long-sleeve linen shirt with rolled sleeves, khaki chinos,
  brown leather belt and sandals, a small bunch of fresh red and green Kampot pepper.
- **Setting:** a pepper farm with vines on wooden poles.

## Rules for new images of him
- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye.
- No conical hats, no text, logos or flags, no real landmarks: never draw the Bokor buildings, the durian statue, the
  town's bridges or roundabouts; use generic scenery. A microphone only while he sings.
