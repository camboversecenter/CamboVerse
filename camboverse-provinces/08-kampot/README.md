## Theara
He is the face of Kampot and can represent the province in anything: he sings its song, guides visitors, answers
questions, and hosts its events, lessons, campaigns and local products. His look is a pepper farmer's linen shirt with
a bunch of fresh Kampot pepper. His guide facts are in [province.md](./province.md); his profile and rules are in
[avatar/profile.md](./avatar/profile.md).
