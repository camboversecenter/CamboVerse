## The province
- In north-west Cambodia, on the northern shore of the Tonle Sap lake.
- Provincial capital: Siem Reap city (ក្រុងសៀមរាប), the gateway to Angkor, with markets, a night market and a
  riverside along the Siem Reap river.
- Home of the **Angkor Archaeological Park**, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, with the temples of the Khmer Empire's
  ancient capitals, managed by the APSARA National Authority. An Angkor pass is needed to visit.

## Places (named in the song)
| Place | What to know |
|---|---|
| Angkor Wat (ប្រាសាទអង្គរវត្ត) | the great 12th-century temple, famous at sunrise and its reflection in the ponds |
| Bayon (ប្រាសាទបាយ័ន) | at the centre of Angkor Thom, with its many smiling stone faces |
| Ta Prohm (តាព្រហ្ម) | temple where giant tree roots grow over the stones |
| Tonle Sap lake (បឹងទន្លេសាប) | Southeast Asia's largest freshwater lake; boat trips to floating and stilt-house villages |
| Night market (ផ្សាររាត្រី) | food, crafts and evening strolls in the city |

Other things to know about: silk farms and weaving workshops, stone and wood carving artisans, Khmer cooking.

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh on National Road 6, roughly 315 km, usually around 5–6 hours (check the current time).
- By air to Siem Reap–Angkor International Airport.

## Responsible travel (he mentions what fits)
- At Angkor and every temple: cover shoulders and knees, speak quietly, never climb on, touch or sit on carvings,
  never take stones. Follow the guards' and APSARA signs.
- Respect monks and active pagodas inside the park.
- Never give money or sweets to children selling or begging; support schools and community projects instead.
- Buy silk, carvings and crafts directly from the people who make them; take rubbish home.
- Wear a life jacket on the Tonle Sap; ask before photographing people in the villages.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
