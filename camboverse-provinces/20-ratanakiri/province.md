## The province
- In Cambodia's far north-east, bordering Laos to the north and Vietnam to the east. The name means "mountain of
  gems" (ទឹកដីត្បូងពេជ្រ in the song).
- Provincial capital: Banlung (ក្រុងបានលុង).
- Landscape: highland plateau with red-earth roads, forests, crater lakes, rivers and waterfalls; coffee, cashew and
  rubber farms.
- People: home to many Indigenous peoples, among them the Tampuan, Jarai, Kreung, Brao and Kavet, together with Khmer
  and Lao communities. Each has its own language, weaving, music (gongs) and customs.
- Nature: Virachey National Park, a large protected area of forest and mountains along the borders.

| Yeak Laom lake (បឹងយក្សឡោម) | a round, clear crater lake in the forest near Banlung, cared for together with the local Indigenous community; swimming and sunsets |

| Cha Ong waterfall (ទឹកជ្រោះចាអ៊ុង) | waterfall near Banlung; check the current access before you go |
| Coffee and rubber farms | the green rows of the highland plantations |
| Evening gong music (ភ្លេងគង) | highland music around the fire, best experienced through community-based tourism |

## Food (named in the song)
- Country food with a highland taste; ask local hosts and community guides what is in season.

## Getting there
- A long road trip from Phnom Penh, roughly 580–600 km, usually a full day (check the current route and time).
- The dry season (about November to April) is easiest; in the rainy season red-earth roads get muddy and waterfalls
  are strongest.

## Responsible travel (she mentions what fits)
- Ask permission before entering a village or photographing people.
- Respect spirit forests, burial grounds and sacred places; follow local guidance.
- Choose community-based tourism and local guides, so visits benefit the people who live there.
- Never buy wildlife or forest products taken illegally. Take rubbish home.
- Take care swimming at waterfalls, especially after rain. Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
