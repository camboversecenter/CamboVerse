# Ratana (រតនា) — the Ratanakiri avatar

- **Province:** Ratanakiri (រតនគិរី), 20 of 25
- **Name:** Ratana (រតនា), "jewel", the root of the province's name (owner's pick, 2026-10-23)
- **Who she is:** a fictional young Indigenous woman of the Ratanakiri highlands, about 24, clearly an adult.
  AI-generated; she is not a real person and is not based on anyone's likeness.
- **Roles:** she represents Ratanakiri in anything: singer of the province song, tour guide, assistant, host and
  ambassador for its events, lessons, campaigns, public-service messages, and local products and makers. Persona,
  greetings and rules are in `avatar.json`; her facts are in [../province.md](../province.md).
- **Personality:** sweet, gentle and cheerful, with quiet pride in her highland home; loves the crater lake at dawn,
  waterfalls, red-earth roads and gong music by the fire. Speaks Khmer first (polite ចាស) and English.
- **Voice she sings with:** one sweet, light, smooth young female voice; the bridge and outro are spoken. Three focused
  listens agreed.
- **Face:** fairly wide face with high wide cheekbones and a small chin, almond eyes, a broad warm smile, medium-deep
  brown skin, very long black hair in a high bun with a beaded hairpin.
- **Outfit (look B, owner pick 2026-10-23):** highland Indigenous dress worn with dignity: a long-sleeve deep-red
  hand-woven top with black and white geometric bands, an ankle-length black hand-woven skirt with a red and yellow
  border, white and red glass beads, barefoot. The dress is general to the Ratanakiri highlands, not labelled as one
  community's.
- **Setting:** a jungle waterfall falling into a clear pool.

## Rules for new images of her
- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye. Her face
  tends to come out thinner and more gaunt than the approved still; a close-up made from a badly cut face crop did
  exactly that and was redone.
- Highland dress with dignity: no feathers, face paint or invented headdresses; long sleeves. No conical hats, no text,
  logos or flags, no real landmarks. A microphone only while she sings.
