# How CamboVerse Provinces meets the DPG Standard

This collection is part of the CamboVerse Digital Public Good. This page goes through the nine indicators of the
[DPG Standard](https://digitalpublicgoods.net/standard/).

| # | Indicator | How we meet it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Relevance to the SDGs** | SDG 4 (Quality Education): free Khmer songs, timed lyrics, karaoke data and a guide avatar per province for learning about Cambodia. SDG 8.9 (sustainable tourism that promotes local culture and products): each avatar is a free tour guide for her or his province, with responsible-travel advice. SDG 11.4 (protect cultural heritage): each province's landscape, crafts and local products are recorded in song and image. |
| 2 | **Open licence** | Media, lyrics and data: CC BY 4.0 ([LICENSE-MEDIA](./LICENSE-MEDIA)). Code: Apache-2.0 ([../LICENSE](../LICENSE)). |
| 3 | **Clear ownership** | Owned and published by the CamboVerse Center. Each province's `song/rights.txt` records the Suno song id, creation date and plan, so anyone can trace the rights. |
| 4 | **Platform independence** | Open formats only: MP3, MP4 (H.264), JPEG/PNG, UTF-8 text, JSON. No file needs a paid tool or a particular platform to use. |
| 5 | **Documentation** | This folder's README, one README per province (avatar profile, credits, how to reuse), `province.md` guide facts, [SKILL.md](./SKILL.md) for using an avatar, and [NOTES.md](./NOTES.md) for how the media was made. |
| 6 | **Non-PII data extraction** | Every file can be downloaded straight from the repository or its GitHub Releases. Lyric timing and karaoke data are plain JSON. |
| 7 | **Privacy and applicable laws** | No personal data. The avatars are fictional, not likenesses of real people, and every face is checked against that rule. Credits name the project, not private individuals. |
| 8 | **Standards and best practices** | Unicode Khmer spelled to the Chuon Nath dictionary, SPDX licence IDs, semantic folder names, loudness at −14 LUFS (the streaming-platform standard). |
| 9 | **Do no harm** | Avatars are clearly adults (18+) in modest clothing. No real landmarks, flags, logos or invented text. Indigenous communities are never shown as costume stereotypes. Every README and caption carries an AI disclosure, and an avatar acting as a guide or assistant always says she or he is an AI. Stories stay hopeful and include no self-harm, violence or unsafe road behaviour. |
