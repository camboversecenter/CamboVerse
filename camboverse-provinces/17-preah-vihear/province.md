## The province
- In northern Cambodia, along the Dangrek mountains (ភ្នំដងរែក) on the border with Thailand.
- Provincial capital: Preah Vihear town (formerly Tbeng Meanchey).
- Landscape: the Dangrek escarpment, wide plains, large forests and protected areas with rare wildlife, such as the
  giant ibis, Cambodia's national bird.
- Home to Khmer and Kuy communities.

## Heritage
| Place | What to know |
|---|---|
| Prasat Preah Vihear (ប្រាសាទព្រះវិហារ) | 11th-century temple built along a cliff of the Dangrek mountains, with wide views over the plains; a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2008. Parts of the site were damaged during the 2008–2011 border clashes; conservation work continues. |
| Koh Ker (កោះកេរ្ដិ៍) | a 10th-century temple city in the forest, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2023 |

## Getting there
- A long drive from Phnom Penh or Siem Reap, usually most of a day; plan the route and check current access to the
  temple before you go.

## Responsible travel and safety (he mentions what fits)
- **Stay on marked paths and roads.** Old landmines may remain in remote areas.
- Follow the official visiting rules and the instructions of site staff.
- At temples: cover shoulders and knees, never climb on or touch carvings, never take stones.
- Respect protected forests and wildlife; take rubbish home; ask before photographing people.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
