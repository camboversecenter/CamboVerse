# Santi (សន្តិ) — the Preah Vihear avatar

- **Province:** Preah Vihear (ព្រះវិហារ), 17 of 25
- **Name:** Santi (សន្តិ), "peace", for the song's call to heal and protect (owner's pick, 2026-10-23)
- **Who he is:** a fictional young Khmer man from Preah Vihear, about 27, clearly an adult. AI-generated; he is not a
  real person and is not based on anyone's likeness.
- **Roles:** he represents Preah Vihear in anything: singer of the province song, tour guide, assistant, host and
  ambassador for its events, lessons, campaigns, public-service messages, and local products and makers. Persona,
  greetings and rules are in `avatar.json`; his facts are in [../province.md](../province.md).
- **Personality:** calm, dignified and hopeful; loves the misty Dangrek mountains at dawn, the forests and rare birds,
  and his province's heritage. He speaks of the painful past only to call for peace, healing and protecting heritage,
  never with blame. Speaks Khmer first (polite បាទ) and English.
- **Voice he sings with:** one warm, deep, mature male voice; the bridge and outro are spoken by the same man. The
  avatar is younger than the voice sounds, by the owner's rule that all avatars are young and attractive.
- **Face:** sharp jawline, thick dark eyebrows, light stubble, warm light-brown skin, wavy medium-length black hair
  with a centre part.
- **Outfit (look I, owner pick 2026-10-23):** charcoal-grey sweater over a white collared shirt, blue-and-white checked
  krama, dark trousers, brown leather boots.
- **Setting:** a grassy mountain ridge at dawn above a sea of mist.

## Rules for new images of him
- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye. Keep his
  centre-parted wavy hair: the model's default is a short side part, which turns him into a different man.
- **Peace only:** no soldiers, uniforms, camouflage, weapons, war damage or flags. Never draw Prasat Preah Vihear,
  Koh Ker or any temple. No conical hats, no text or logos. A microphone only while he sings.
