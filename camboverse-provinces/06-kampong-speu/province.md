## The province
- In central-western Cambodia, just west of Phnom Penh, rising to the Cardamom foothills.
- Provincial capital: Chbar Mon (ក្រុងច្បារមន).
- National Road 4 runs through it, lined with tall sugar palms.
- Known for **Kampong Speu palm sugar** (ស្ករត្នោតកំពង់ស្ពឺ), which has Geographical Indication (GI) protection, and
  for the cool pine forests of Kirirom.

## Places (named in the song)
| Place | What to know |
|---|---|
| Kirirom (គិរីរម្យ) | a national park on a cool plateau with pine forests, viewpoints and camping; **official name and rules to verify** |
| Chambak waterfall (ទឹកធ្លាក់ចំបក់) | a waterfall in the Kirirom area where people swim and picnic; take care in the rainy season |
| Phnom Aural (ភ្នំឱរ៉ាល់) | Cambodia's highest mountain (about 1,800 m), in a wildlife sanctuary; climb only with a local guide |
| Te Tuk Pus hot spring (តេទឹកពុះ) | a natural hot spring near Aural; **location and access to verify** |
| Sugar-palm villages | families climb the palms, collect the sap and cook it into palm sugar |

## Food (named in the song)
- **Palm sugar (ស្ករត្នោត)** and fresh, sweet palm juice (ទឹកត្នោតផ្អែមថ្មី, the non-alcoholic kind).

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh on National Road 4, roughly 50 km to Chbar Mon (about 1–1.5 hours) and about 2.5–3 hours to
  Kirirom (check the current time).

## Responsible travel (he mentions what fits)
- In the national park and wildlife sanctuary: stay on trails, camp and make fires only where allowed, never cut trees,
  hunt or buy wildlife.
- Take great care at waterfalls and hot springs.
- Buy palm sugar directly from the families who make it.
- Take all rubbish home; ask before photographing people; wear a helmet on a motorbike.
