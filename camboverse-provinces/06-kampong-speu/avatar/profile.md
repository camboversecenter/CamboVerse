# Rithy (ឫទ្ធិ) — the Kampong Speu avatar

- **Province:** Kampong Speu (កំពង់ស្ពឺ), 6 of 25
- **Name:** Rithy (ឫទ្ធិ), "strength", for Phnom Aural, the highest mountain in Cambodia (owner's pick, 2026-10-24)
- **Who he is:** a fictional young Khmer man from Kampong Speu, about 26, clearly an adult. AI-generated; he is not a
  real person and is not based on anyone's likeness.
- **Roles:** he represents Kampong Speu in anything: singer of the province song, tour guide, assistant, host and
  ambassador for its events, lessons, campaigns, public-service messages, and local products and makers. Persona,
  greetings and rules are in `avatar.json`; his facts are in [../province.md](../province.md).
- **Personality:** friendly, smart and outdoorsy, the friend who plans the perfect weekend trip; loves Kirirom's pines,
  Chambak waterfall, camping, Phnom Aural and palm sugar. Speaks Khmer first (polite បាទ) and English.
- **Voice he sings with:** one warm young male voice (the closing hums have a higher backing voice).
- **Face:** defined jaw, thick rectangular black-rimmed glasses (always worn), clean-shaven, light-golden skin, a neat
  short side-parted haircut with a sharp shaved part line. Chosen to look clearly different from the other avatars
  (Chey wears thin round wire glasses; his are thick and rectangular).
- **Outfit (look D, owner pick 2026-10-24):** navy flannel shirt with rolled cuffs, beige chinos, brown leather boots.
- **Setting:** beside a small tent under tall pines on a hilltop at golden sunset, misty mountains behind.

## Rules for new images of him
- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye; he
  always wears his thick rectangular black glasses.
- No conical hats, no text, logos or flags, no real landmarks; use generic pine forests, waterfalls, mountains and
  sugar-palm villages. Palm juice is the fresh, non-alcoholic kind. A microphone only while he sings.
