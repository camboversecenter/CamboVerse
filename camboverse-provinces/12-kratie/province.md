## The province
- In north-eastern Cambodia, on both banks of the Mekong.
- Provincial capital: Kratie town (ក្រុងក្រចេះ), a quiet riverside town known for its sunsets over the Mekong and its
  old wooden and shuttered houses.
- Home to some of the last **Irrawaddy dolphins** (ផ្សោត) of the Mekong, a critically endangered species.

## Places (named in the song)
| Place | What to know |
|---|---|
| Kampi (កាំពី) | a deep pool of the Mekong north of Kratie town where local boatmen take visitors to watch the dolphins |
| Koh Trong (កោះទ្រង់) | a river island opposite Kratie town, reached by ferry; quiet lanes for cycling, village houses, pomelo orchards |
| Wat Sorsor Moi Roi (វត្តសសរមួយរយ), the "100-pillar pagoda" | a pagoda in Sambor district, north of Kratie town; a place of worship (do not draw it in images) |
| Riverside at sunset | the Mekong waterfront of Kratie town |

## Food (named in the song)
- **Krolan (ក្រឡាន)** from Thma Kre (ថ្មគ្រែ): sticky rice with coconut milk and beans, roasted in bamboo.
- **Pomelo (ក្រូចថ្លុង)** from Koh Trong.

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh, roughly 300 km north-east, usually around 5–6 hours (check the current time and road).

## Responsible travel (he mentions what fits)
- The dolphins are critically endangered: go with local licensed boatmen, keep a distance, keep quiet, never chase,
  feed or touch them, and never throw rubbish into the river.
- Wear a life jacket on boats and ferries.
- At pagodas: dress modestly, take shoes off where asked.
- Buy food and crafts directly from the people who make them; take rubbish home; ask before photographing people.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
