## The province
- In north-western Cambodia, bordering Thailand.
- Provincial capital: Serei Saophoan (ក្រុងសិរីសោភ័ណ), often called Sisophon.
- Wide flat rice plains with a few low hills, reservoirs and wetlands.
- Known for the temple of **Banteay Chhmar**, for the **sarus cranes** of Ang Trapeang Thmor, and for village weaving.

## Places (named in the song)
| Place | What to know |
|---|---|
| Banteay Chhmar (ប្រាសាទបន្ទាយឆ្មារ) | a large Angkorian temple of the late 12th–early 13th century (reign of Jayavarman VII), known for its face towers and long bas-reliefs, including many-armed Lokeshvara (លោកេស្វរ); local community homestays and guides; do not draw it in images |
| Ang Trapeang Thmor (អាងត្រពាំងថ្ម) | a large reservoir and protected area where sarus cranes (ក្រៀល) and many water birds gather, best seen in the dry season with a local guide |
| Phnom Bak / Phnom Chonhcheang (ភ្នំបាក់ ភ្នំជញ្ជាំង) | hills named in the song for sunsets; **names and location to verify** |
| Weaving villages | silk and krama woven on hand looms under the houses |

## Food (named in the song)
- Country cooking (ម្ហូបស្រុកស្រែ) of the north-west.

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh, roughly 360 km north-west, usually around 6–7 hours; Sisophon is about 1.5–2 hours from
  Siem Reap (check the current time). Banteay Chhmar is further north of Sisophon.

## Responsible travel (he mentions what fits)
- At ancient temples: never climb on fallen stones or carvings, never touch the reliefs or take anything, follow the
  signs and site staff.
- At pagodas: dress modestly, take shoes off where asked.
- Watch the cranes from a distance with a local guide; never chase or feed them.
- Buy silk and krama directly from the weavers; take rubbish home; ask before photographing people.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
