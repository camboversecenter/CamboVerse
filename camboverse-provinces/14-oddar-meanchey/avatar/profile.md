- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye. Image

- No conical hats, no text, logos or flags, no real landmarks, no border posts, soldiers or weapons; use generic
  mountains, lakes, forests and markets. A microphone only while he sings.
