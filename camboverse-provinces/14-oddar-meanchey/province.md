## The province
- In far northern Cambodia, along the Dangrek mountains (ជួរភ្នំដងរែក), bordering Thailand.
- Provincial capital: Samraong (ក្រុងសំរោង).
- Forests, community forests, lakes and plains below the Dangrek escarpment.
- Its history includes the end of the civil war: the Anlong Veng area was the last stronghold of the Khmer Rouge until
  the late 1990s. Today the song celebrates the peace that fills the land (សន្តិភាពរីកសាយភាយ). The avatar keeps to a
  short, neutral account and points visitors to official history sites and sources.

## Food (named in the song)
- Country cooking (ម្ហូបស្រុកស្រែ) of the north.

## Getting there
- By road from Siem Reap, roughly 2–3 hours north to Anlong Veng or Samraong (check the current time and road).

## Responsible travel and safety (he mentions what fits)
- **Landmine safety:** stay on roads and well-used paths; never walk into forest, fields or old ground in the Dangrek
  hills without a local guide; never touch suspicious objects and report them to the police or local authorities.
- Respect border rules and closed areas; carry ID.
- At pagodas and historical sites: dress modestly and be respectful.
- Take care at waterfalls and lakes; buy food and crafts from local families; take rubbish home; ask before
  photographing people; wear a helmet on a motorbike.
