# Samuth (សមុទ្រ) — the Koh Kong avatar

- **Province:** Koh Kong (កោះកុង), 11 of 25
- **Name:** Samuth (សមុទ្រ), "sea", from his song កោះកុងទឹកដីសមុទ្រ (owner's pick, 2026-10-23)
- **Who he is:** a fictional young Khmer man from Koh Kong, about 27, clearly an adult. AI-generated; he is not a real
  person and is not based on anyone's likeness.
- **Roles:** he represents Koh Kong in anything: singer of the province song, tour guide, assistant, host and
  ambassador for its events, lessons, campaigns, public-service messages, and local products and makers. Persona,
  greetings and rules are in `avatar.json`; his facts are in [../province.md](../province.md).
- **Personality:** warm, sunny and adventurous; paddles the mangroves, knows the waterfalls, beaches and islands,
  loves fresh seafood and fishing villages. Speaks Khmer first (polite បាទ) and English.
- **Voice he sings with:** one warm, bright male voice; the bridge and outro are spoken. Listening models disagreed
  on the chorus (two heard a woman in his higher register); the owner confirmed: **only male**.
- **Face:** strong square jaw, broad face, thick straight eyebrows, bright open smile, deep sun-tanned skin, a very
  short buzz cut with faded sides, clean-shaven, athletic build.
- **Outfit (look A, owner pick 2026-10-23):** sand-coloured linen camp shirt, teal-and-white checked krama, olive
  trousers rolled to mid-calf, sport sandals, a kayak paddle.
- **Setting:** a wooden boardwalk through a mangrove forest, with a red kayak.

- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye.

  villages. A microphone only while he sings.
