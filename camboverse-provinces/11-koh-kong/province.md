## The province
- In Cambodia's south-west, on the Gulf of Thailand coast, bordering Thailand.
- Provincial capital: Khemarak Phoumin town (ក្រុងខេមរភូមិន្ទ).
- Where the mountains meet the sea: the Cardamom Mountains, one of the largest rainforests in mainland Southeast Asia,
  run down to mangroves, rivers, beaches and islands.
- Protected areas include the Southern Cardamom and Botum Sakor national parks and the Peam Krasop mangroves.
- Community-based ecotourism lets visitors stay with villages in the forest and on the coast.

## Food (named in the song)
- Fresh seafood: fish, crab and more, straight from the fishing villages.

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh, usually around 5 hours (check the current route and time).

## Responsible travel (he mentions what fits)
- Wear a life jacket on boats and kayaks; swim only where it is safe, never at waterfalls after heavy rain.
- Stay on boardwalks and trails in mangroves and rainforest; never buy wildlife or illegal forest products.
- Choose community-based tourism and local guides; take rubbish home, especially plastic near the sea.
- Ask before photographing people. Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
