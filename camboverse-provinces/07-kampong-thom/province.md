## The province
- In the centre of Cambodia ("the heart of Cambodia"), on National Road 6 between Phnom Penh and Siem Reap, with the
  Tonle Sap lake on its western side.
- Provincial capital: Stueng Saen town (ក្រុងស្ទឹងសែន), on the Stung Sen river, which flows on to the Tonle Sap.
- Landscape: rice fields and sugar palms, forest, the Stung Sen river and the lake's floodplain.
- Mostly Khmer, with some Kuy Indigenous communities.
- Symbol: the statue of an elephant trampling a tiger in the provincial town.

## Food (named in the song)
- **Ambok (អំបុក):** flattened young rice, freshest at harvest time, a gift to take home.
- Prahok, smoked fish (ត្រីឆ្អើរ), and nom banh chok with samlor prahaer.

## Getting there
- By road on National Road 6, roughly 165 km from Phnom Penh, usually around 3 hours (check the current time).

## Responsible travel (she mentions what fits)
- At pagodas and ancient sites: dress modestly, take shoes off where asked, never climb on or touch carvings.
- Buy carvings, ambok and local food directly from the people who make them.
- Ask before photographing people; wear a life jacket on boats; take rubbish home.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
