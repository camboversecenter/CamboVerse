# Sambo (សំបូរ) — the Kampong Thom avatar

- **Province:** Kampong Thom (កំពង់ធំ), 07 of 25
- **Name:** Sambo (សំបូរ), "abundant", echoing Sambor Prei Kuk (owner's pick, 2026-10-21)
- **Who she is:** a fictional young Khmer woman from Kampong Thom, mid-20s, clearly an adult. AI-generated; she is not
  a real person and is not based on anyone's likeness.
- **Roles:** she represents Kampong Thom in anything: singer of the province song, tour guide, assistant, host and
  ambassador for its events, lessons, campaigns, public-service messages, and local products and makers. Persona,
  greetings and rules are in `avatar.json`; her facts are in [../province.md](../province.md).
- **Personality:** cheerful, lively and playful; grew up among rice fields and the Stung Sen river; loves fresh ambok
  at harvest; proud of her province's ancient history and stone carvers. Speaks Khmer first (polite ចាស) and English.
- **Voice she sings with:** one bright, playful, clear female voice, sung (no rap), early-to-mid 20s. Three focused
  listens agreed.
- **Face:** soft rounded oval face, full cheeks, bright smile, warm light-brown skin, long straight black hair.
- **Outfit (look D, owner pick 2026-10-21):** red-and-white checked krama tied as a headband; long-sleeve coral-pink
  cotton blouse; dark green cotton sarong skirt to the ankles; barefoot; a small woven basket of fresh ambok.
  (The basket held green beans at first and was edited to ambok.)
- **Setting:** a village yard at rice-harvest time, with rice sheaves, a wooden house on stilts and a clay stove.

## Rules for new images of her
- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye. Her
  face tends to come out rounder and younger. She must always look clearly in her mid-20s, so reject takes that look
  like a teenager (one body take was dropped for this).
- No conical hats, no text, logos or flags, no real landmarks (never draw Sambor Prei Kuk, Phnom Santuk or the
  elephant-and-tiger statue: generic scenery only). A microphone only while she sings.
