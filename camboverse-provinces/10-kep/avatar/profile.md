# Malis (មាលីស) — the Kep avatar

- **Province:** Kep (កែប), 10 of 25
- **Name:** Malis (មាលីស), "jasmine", a fresh flower name to go with her hibiscus (owner's pick, 2026-10-23)
- **Who she is:** a fictional young Khmer woman from Kep, about 24, clearly an adult. AI-generated; she is not a real
  person and is not based on anyone's likeness.
- **Roles:** she represents Kep in anything: singer of the province song, tour guide, assistant, host and ambassador
  for its events, lessons, campaigns, public-service messages, and local products and makers. Persona, greetings and
  rules are in `avatar.json`; her facts are in [../province.md](../province.md).
- **Personality:** bright, cheerful and playful, with a sunny holiday mood; loves the crab market, crab with green
  pepper, boat rides to the islands, hill walks and sunsets with family. Speaks Khmer first (polite ចាស) and English.
- **Voice she sings with:** one bright, clear, playful young female voice; the bridge and outro are spoken. Eight
  listens across two models agreed.
- **Face:** oval face, sweet warm smile, a small beauty mark on her chin, medium-brown skin, long black hair in a side
  fishtail braid with a red hibiscus behind her right ear, small round gold earrings.
- **Outfit (look D, owner pick 2026-10-23):** sea-blue long-sleeve Khmer silk blouse, white-and-blue silk sampot with
  a fine silver border, barefoot.
- **Setting:** a beachfront wooden deck at sunset, with crab and green peppercorns on the table.

## Rules for new images of her
- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye,
  including the beauty mark on her chin.
- Modest: no swimwear. No conical hats, no text, logos or flags, no real landmarks: never draw the crab statue or
  other Kep monuments; use generic beaches, markets and hills. A microphone only while she sings.
