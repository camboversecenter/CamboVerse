## The province
- One of Cambodia's smallest provinces, on the south coast next to Kampot and close to the Vietnamese border.
- A seaside town since the early 20th century, when it was built as a resort; today known for its relaxed coast,
  seafood and islands.
- Famous for **crab with green Kampot pepper** (ក្ដាមឆាម្រេចខ្ចី) and its crab market.

## Places (named in the song)
| Place | What to know |
|---|---|
| Crab market (ផ្សារក្ដាម) | fresh crab and seafood straight from the boats, cooked on the spot |
| Crab statue (រូបសំណាកក្ដាម) | a big crab statue in the sea, the symbol of Kep |
| Kep hill / Kep National Park (ភ្នំកែប) | forest trails with views over the sea and islands |
| Rabbit Island (កោះទន្សាយ) | a quiet island a short boat ride away, with simple beaches |
| Coral (ផ្កាថ្ម) | coral and sea life around the islands; look, never touch |

## Food (named in the song)
- Crab stir-fried with fresh green Kampot pepper, and other fresh seafood.

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh, roughly 160–170 km, usually around 3–4 hours (check the current time).

## Responsible travel (she mentions what fits)
- Wear a life jacket on boats to the islands; swim only where it is safe.
- Never stand on or take coral or shells from the reef. Stay on the trails in the national park.
- Buy seafood from local sellers; take rubbish home, especially plastic near the sea; ask before photographing people.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
