# Phirun (ភិរុណ) — the Stung Treng avatar

- **Province:** Stung Treng (ស្ទឹងត្រែង), 22 of 25
- **Name:** Phirun (ភិរុណ), "rain", for the rivers, flooded forest and waterfalls (owner's pick, 2026-10-23)
- **Who he is:** a fictional young Khmer man from Stung Treng, about 26, clearly an adult. AI-generated; he is not a
  real person and is not based on anyone's likeness.
- **Roles:** he represents Stung Treng in anything: singer of the province song, tour guide, assistant, host and
  ambassador for its events, lessons, campaigns, public-service messages, and local products and makers. Persona,
  greetings and rules are in `avatar.json`; his facts are in [../province.md](../province.md).
- **Personality:** calm, curious and gently adventurous; loves the flooded forest, the waterfalls, sunsets over the
  river islands and fresh Mekong fish. Speaks Khmer first (polite បាទ) and English.
- **Voice he sings with:** one warm young male voice, with shouted backing ad-libs. 7 of 9 focused listens (three
  models) heard a man.
- **Face:** narrow V-shaped face with prominent cheekbones, clean-shaven, warm golden-tan skin, straight black
  shoulder-length hair worn loose with a slim woven brown-and-cream headband. Chosen to look clearly different from the
  other avatars.
- **Outfit (look C, owner pick 2026-10-23):** white short-sleeve cotton shirt, khaki cargo trousers, river sandals,
  canvas shoulder bag.
- **Setting:** flat rocks before a wide forest waterfall pouring over rock ledges.

## Rules for new images of him
- Send `face.jpg` with every prompt (plus `body.jpg` only for the default outfit) and compare the face by eye. Keep
  his long hair; the headband may be left off for formal looks, the face stays the same.
- No conical hats, no text, logos or flags, no real landmarks: never draw Preah Nimith waterfall or the Ramsar site as
  if real, and do not draw dolphins; use generic waterfalls, flooded forests and river islands. A microphone only
  while he sings.
