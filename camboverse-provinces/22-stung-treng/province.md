## The province
- In north-eastern Cambodia, bordering Laos, on the Mekong.
- Provincial capital: Stung Treng town (ក្រុងស្ទឹងត្រែង), where the Sekong river joins the Mekong.
- Called the land of **four rivers** (ទន្លេបួន): the Mekong, Sekong, Sesan and Srepok all flow through the province.
- The **Stung Treng Ramsar Site** protects a stretch of the Mekong with flooded forest, channels, islands and rapids,
  home to many fish and birds.

## Getting there
- By road from Phnom Penh, roughly 450 km north, usually a full day by car or bus (check the current time and road).

## Responsible travel (he mentions what fits)
- The flooded forest is a protected wetland: go with local boatmen or community guides; never cut trees, fish with
  illegal gear, or disturb birds and dolphins; take all rubbish back.
- Wear a life jacket on boats; take great care at waterfalls and rapids and never swim near strong currents.
- At pagodas: dress modestly, take shoes off where asked.
- Buy fish and food from local families; ask before photographing people.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
