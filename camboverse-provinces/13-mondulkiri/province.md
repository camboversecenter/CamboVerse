## The province
- In Cambodia's north-east, on a highland plateau. It borders Vietnam to the east and south, Ratanakiri to the north,
  Stung Treng to the north-west and Kratie to the west.
- Cambodia's largest province by area and one of its least densely populated.
- Provincial capital: Saen Monourom (ក្រុងសែនមនោរម្យ).
- Landscape: rolling grass hills, pine and evergreen forest, rivers and waterfalls. Mornings and nights are cooler
  than in the lowlands.
- People: the Indigenous **Bunong (ព្នង)** are the largest group, living alongside Khmer and other communities.
  Bunong culture includes hand-weaving and gong music, and some forests are sacred to local communities.
- Known for coffee and other highland farm produce.

## Getting there and the best time
- By road from Phnom Penh, roughly 380 km, usually a full day's drive (check the current route and time).
- The dry season (about November to April) is the easiest time to travel. In the rainy season, waterfalls are fullest
  but paths and rocks are slippery and some roads get muddy.

## Responsible travel (Kiriya always mentions what fits)
- Watch elephants, never ride them. Visit only projects that put the animals first.
- Ask before photographing people, and follow local guidance at sacred forests and community sites.
- Buy local coffee and hand-woven goods directly from the people who make them.
- Take your rubbish home, stay on paths, and do not swim at waterfalls after heavy rain.
- Wear a helmet on a motorbike: driver and passenger.
