# Published illustrative heritage models

These site models are original, simplified Blender illustrations made for the
CamboVerse Center at the National University of Management. The models are
licensed under CC BY 4.0 with attribution to CamboVerse Center / NUM. They are
not scans, surveyed reconstructions, restoration proposals, or visitor maps.
Design references informed the geometry; third-party photographs and meshes
are not embedded in these GLBs. The editable Blender scenes, reference images,
renders, build reports, and real 3DGS capture sources remain local.

`manifest.json` records the 30 Normal/Ultra GLBs promoted for the 14 map
destinations beyond Wat Phnom and the separate Kulen waterfall scene. The
already-published Wat Phnom pair remains in `wat-phnom-v4/`. The two previously
published Wat Phnom capture views are separate and unchanged by this release.
The Sambor Prei Kuk Ultra GLB is repacked losslessly so it fits Cloudflare's
per-file asset limit; the decoded mesh bufferViews are verified against the
source export. `scripts/publish-heritage-assets.mjs --copy` reproduces this set.

See `docs/HERITAGE-BLENDER-V1.md` and site-specific review notes for the
illustrative scope, references, geometry checks, and remaining device tests.
