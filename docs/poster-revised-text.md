## Two Public Goods

### Open Khmer Heritage Archive

**Original:**
> Openly licensed 3D capture data (Gaussian splats, glTF meshes, textures and provenance) that anyone can build on.

**Revised:**
> Free 3D scans of Khmer heritage — open for schools, researchers, and developers everywhere.

---

### CamboVerse Viewer / Platform

**Original:**
> The open-source experience layer that renders the archive in any browser: mobile first, low bandwidth.

**Revised:**
> The free viewer that opens the archive in any browser. Built for mobile and slow connections.

## Design Principles (6 items)

| # | Original | Revised |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Runs on a ~$150 Android phone over 4G. A hard requirement. | Works on any cheap phone, even on slow 4G. |
| 2 | Zero install. Opens from a Facebook or Telegram link. | No download. Just tap a link on Facebook or Telegram. |
| 3 | Immersive in a headset via WebXR; never required. | Also works in a VR headset — but you don't need one. |
| 4 | glTF · WebGL · WebGPU · WebXR. No vendor lock-in. | Built on open standards. No lock-in. |
| 5 | Building CamboVerse is the curriculum. | NUM students build it as part of their studies. |
| 6 | Heritage captured with consent and cultural care. | Sites captured respectfully, with community consent. |
