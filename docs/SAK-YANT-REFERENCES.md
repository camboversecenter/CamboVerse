# Sak Yant collection: artwork and source notes

Updated 26 October 2026. Covers all six existing gallery entries, preserving their IDs and achievements.

## What ships

Four original vector studies in `src/sakyantArtwork.ts` and two replacement tattoo illustrations in `public/art/sak-yant/`, shared by the gallery, the skin-swatch/camera compositor and SVG downloads. The tiger and naga were replaced after the user rejected the first simplified drawings; see [the v3 animal notes](SAK-YANT-ANIMALS-V3.md) for references, prompts and provenance. Their SVG downloads embed raster artwork rather than claiming vector paths. No remote image, font, Blender model or additional runtime dependency is required. The artwork is the same across device quality modes. Native paths and decoded ink masks are cached; the preview decal is drawn once per motif/ink change.

The drawing geometry and SVG exports are CC-BY-4.0, attributed to CamboVerse contributors; renderer code remains Apache-2.0. External photographs and artists' drawings have **not** been copied into the repository or relicensed. Links below are references, not licensed asset sources or endorsements.

The tiger/naga entries below record the first vector-study research. Their current artwork and current primary visual references are superseded by [the v3 animal notes](SAK-YANT-ANIMALS-V3.md).

| Entry | Reference | Supported observation and limits |

The Center for Khmer Studies catalogue lists *Yantra et mantra = យ័ន្ត និង មន្ត*, text and photographs by Olivier de Bernon ([catalogue](https://library.khmerstudies.org/bib/7334)). The book itself was **not** consulted; it is a possible next reference for a practitioner-led review, not evidence for the current drawings.

## Editorial boundaries

- Gao Yord, Hah Taew and Paed Tidt are regional Thai names; do not present this mixed collection as six certified Khmer patterns.
- No generated pseudo-Khom writing. Sacred inscriptions are omitted and this status is visible in the gallery, expanded information, preview and exported image.
- Meanings describe practitioner beliefs, not promised protection, wealth or medical effects.
- Khmer labels and cultural interpretations remain drafts requiring native/practitioner review. No master approval has been obtained. Accurate inscriptions require a documented source and competent reading; permission is also needed before distributing another artist's rendition in this open archive.
- The preview starts on a skin-coloured swatch. Camera activation is explicit and optional; frames stay on the device. This is a flat placement preview, not body tracking.

## Review checklist

Select all six named thumbnails; check the complete silhouette and enlarged linework. Try black/red ink, open Learn more and export an SVG. Check that the exported caption retains the study status. In the swatch preview, drag, resize, rotate, reset and make a snapshot. On a physical phone, also test portrait/landscape and camera permission denial, activation and closing. No physical-phone or camera hardware validation is claimed by desktop checks.
