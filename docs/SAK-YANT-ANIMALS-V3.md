# Tiger and naga tattoo illustrations — v3

26 October 2026. Replaces the rejected simple standing tiger and smiling cobra-like naga. The other four motifs retain their vector drawings.

## References actually viewed

- [Sak-Yant.com — Yant Suea Phaen](https://www.sak-yant.com/sak-yant/about-sacred-yant/yantsueapaen1/) and its [drawing](https://www.sak-yant.com/wp-content/uploads/2009/02/yantsueapaen1.jpg): inspected the animal proportions, curved tail, stripe rhythm and distinction between animal illustration and surrounding sacred frame. The new crouching three-quarter tiger does not trace this pose or copy the inscriptions.
- [Bangkok Tattoo Studio 13 — naga designs](https://www.bkktattoostudio13.com/sak-yant-snake-naga-tattoos-designs-meaning.html) and its [naga illustration](https://www.bkktattoostudio13.com/assets/images/sak-yant-tattoo-design-naga-12.webp): inspected the scale work, pointed crest, elongated muzzle and coiled silhouette. The studio explicitly describes some single-naga examples as modern. This reference must not be relabelled a historical Khmer pattern. No studio lettering, logos, gold squares or mantras are reproduced.

## Assets and integration

- `public/art/sak-yant/tiger-v3.webp` — 1254 × 1254, 401,974 bytes.
- `public/art/sak-yant/naga-v3.webp` — 1254 × 1254, 342,680 bytes.
- Created with the built-in image generation tool, not Blender or the API/CLI fallback. Original AI-assisted compositions informed by references; not practitioner-approved sacred tattoo stencils.
- WebP compression preserves source transparency. On-device Canvas converts luminance to ink opacity once, so white interior areas reveal the skin swatch. Black/red colourisation is done from that same cached mask; the gallery, preview and exports share the new assets.
- Versioned URLs prevent old cached artwork from being selected. Rejected vector animal functions were removed, with explicit loading/retry UI instead of silent fallback.
- SVG downloads are self-contained but embed a raster PNG. This is disclosed in the file metadata and expanded information.
- No new network service or package dependency. Both drawings total 744,654 bytes. These are original project illustrations offered under the archive's CC-BY-4.0 terms with attribution to CamboVerse contributors; third-party reference images remain with their owners and are not distributed.

## Final generation prompts

### tiger

Create one highly accomplished BLACK INK TATTOO FLASH DRAWING of a single tiger for the CamboVerse cultural learning gallery. The recent reference screenshots show a naga tattoo and a traditional regional tiger yantra: use them ONLY as visual research for the discipline of line work, NOT as compositions to trace or reproduce. Design an ORIGINAL tiger, fully visible, in a tense LOW CROUCH, body three-quarter side profile, front paws reaching to the left, muscular shoulders, bent haunches, an expressive snarling feline muzzle facing slightly toward viewer, small rounded ears, real feline cheek anatomy, visible fangs, sweeping raised tail. Four anatomically coherent legs with articulated toes and claws. Dense tapered BLACK tiger stripes follow anatomy, strong bold outer contour, refined fine internal linework and sparse controlled engraving hatch. Contemporary hand-drawn Southeast Asian tattoo-flash craft, elegant ornamental rhythm, high-end tattoo studio quality. Not a cute animal icon, not a cartoon, not a mascot. No generic standing quadruped. Pure opaque black strokes and black filled shapes with clean transparent negative spaces; no white fills, no grey wash, no skin photograph, no paper, no shadows. Transparent background. Square canvas, animal fills about 85% width with generous safety margin, all tail/claws inside frame. No text, no sacred lettering, no imitation script, no logos, no watermark, no surrounding yantra frame. This is a reference-informed illustrative tattoo motif, not a certified traditional sacred pattern.

### naga

Create one exquisitely detailed BLACK INK TATTOO FLASH DRAWING of a single Khmer/Southeast-Asian-inspired NAGA serpent for the CamboVerse cultural learning gallery. Recent reference screenshots show a coiled naga tattoo and a regional tiger yantra: study the NAGA for refined scales and pointed flame crest, but create an ORIGINAL pose/composition, not a tracing. One long legless mythical serpent, a proud side-profile head facing left at upper third, fierce elongated reptilian muzzle, slightly open jaw with clear curved fangs, narrowed reptile eye, layered pointed flame-like crown and elegant whisker flourishes. NO human face, no smiling cobra face, no round ears. Serpentine neck flows down through TWO broad elegant alternating coils, logically continuous scaled body, visible clear overlapping turns, narrow tapered tail finishing in a graceful curl. Repeated meticulous overlapping fish-like scales align along curved body, banded belly plates on inner curve, narrow dorsal ornament; alternating bold silhouette strokes and fine ornamental contour details, high-end professional tattoo flash line drawing. Crisp dense black ink, flowing balanced composition, legible negative spaces at small size. No Chinese dragon limbs or wings. Pure opaque black strokes and black filled shapes with transparent negative spaces; no white fills, no grey wash, no paper, no background, no skin photograph, no shadows. Transparent background, square canvas, animal fills 85% height with generous safety margin and full crest/tail visible. No text, no sacred lettering, no imitation script, no logos, no watermark, no gold squares, no surrounding yantra symbols. This is a reference-informed illustrative motif, not a certified Khmer sacred pattern.
