# Humerus study pilot: external, internal and microscopic

Review-only Blender assets generated by `scripts/build-humerus-study-v1.py`.
No live app asset is replaced. All outputs use `public/models/local/humerus-study-v1-*`.

## Three separate representations

- Macro: external humerus-like form with tagged head, shaft, tubercles,
  deltoid tuberosity, distal body, epicondyles, capitulum and trochlea.
  Landmark pieces form an illustrative assembly, not a single watertight sculpt.
- Cutaway: generic long-bone shaft/end schematic with compact bone, endosteum,
  periosteum, yellow/red marrow, spongy bone and vessels. It is NOT a precise
  boolean section through the macro humerus mesh. The medullary cavity is the
  space occupied by marrow, not an extra solid organ.
- Micro: enlarged osteon with concentric lamellae, central canal wall, vessels,
  nerve, lacunae, osteocytes and canaliculi. Cells and microchannels are exaggerated
  for visibility. Dark lacuna markers are diagrammatic spaces, not extra tissue.

Both tiers keep identical part IDs and semantic structures. Normal uses fewer
segments. No transmission or texture-download dependency is introduced.
Micro assets carry `studyScale=magnified-not-body-scale`; any future UI MUST
display "Magnified — not to body scale". No physical micro-scale measurement
or numerical magnification factor is claimed.

## Review and future integration

The first review must assess recognizable external proportions, cutaway clarity,
and micro-view readability. Detailed joint surfaces/fossae, accurate trabecular
orientation, complete osteon networks and tissue distributions remain incomplete.
These are not clinically validated models. Do not present them as scan-derived.

After visual approval, add an on-demand three-level specimen viewer rather than
loading microgeometry into the whole-body scene. Selection, isolation, information
cards and quiz mapping are a later app integration step, not implemented here.
