# Blender eye pilot (local only)

Original geometry and vertex colors generated by `scripts/generate-lab-eye-pilot.py`.
No external meshes, photographs, textures, or add-ons are downloaded. This is a
schematic teaching asset, not a scan or a medically validated anatomical model.
The implementation follows the repository's Apache-2.0 code licence; generated
pilot content is offered under CC-BY-4.0, attribution: CamboVerse contributors.

## Rebuild and check

```powershell
& 'C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2\blender.exe' --background --factory-startup --python scripts/generate-lab-eye-pilot.py
node scripts/check-lab-eye-pilot.mjs
npm run typecheck
```

Use a fresh background Blender process: the generator clears its current scene.
Files are written to the ignored `public/models/local/` folder:
`lab-eye-normal.glb`, `lab-eye-ultra.glb`, `lab-eye-pilot.blend`, and stats JSON.
The saved Blender scene shows the exterior; internal objects can be unhidden in
the Outliner. The web renderer sets layer visibility and transparent materials.

## Content and rendering

Both assets have the same seven lesson IDs: sclera, cornea, iris, lens, retina,
optic-nerve and vitreous. Shell halves allow a physical cutaway. Optics shows the
lens, retina and nerve. The colored iris has a dilation morph; the black pupil
disc matches its opening in Whole mode and is hidden in Cutaway to expose the lens.
Brightness drives the morph; Stop freezes it. Cornea is selectable at its rim or
from the notes. Vitreous is selected from the notes so its transparent surface does
not intercept clicks intended for structures behind it.

- Normal: approximately 7,426 triangles / 175 KB; no shadows or transmission.
  The dome uses inexpensive alpha transparency rather than optical refraction.
- Ultra/VR: approximately 36,580 triangles / 823 KB; denser iris fibers and
  geometry, shadows and a transmissive cornea.
- Each file has 12 meshes, but hidden lesson-layer meshes do not draw. No texture
  downloads. Files remain uncompressed pending final asset pipeline review.
- Eye orientation is front +Z, matching the existing label anchors. The original
  procedural renderer remains the production, test, loading and error fallback.

The smoke check verifies importability, finite geometry, lesson-part parity,
size budgets, vertex colors and dilation endpoints. Browser checks cover quality
switching, Whole/Cutaway/Optics, brightness and selection. Educator, low-end Android
over 4G, and headset review remain required before production adoption.
