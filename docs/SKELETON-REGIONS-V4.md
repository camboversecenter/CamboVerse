# Skeleton regions V4 review

Research: OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology 2e, 7.1 (axial/appendicular
divisions), 8.2 (upper limb), 8.4 (lower limb).

- https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/7-1-divisions-of-the-skeletal-system
- https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-2-bones-of-the-upper-limb
- https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/8-4-bones-of-the-lower-limb

Preserves the approved skull/pelvis source geometry and V3 torso/long bones.
Replaces coarse hands/feet: eight wrist pieces, metacarpals and separated
phalange segments; seven hind/midfoot pieces, metatarsals and toe segments.
Thumbs are lateral in the palms-forward pose, with two phalange segments.
Individual carpal/tarsal shapes remain simplified, not anatomically validated.

Thirteen named export regions: skull, spine, rib cage, shoulder girdle, pelvis,
left/right arms, hands, legs and feet. These are app-oriented learning regions,
not a strict bone-by-bone anatomical taxonomy (pelvis includes the sacrum).
`anatomyPart=skeleton` is retained for backward compatibility and
`skeletonRegion` records the region on each group.

Generated by `scripts/build-skeleton-regions-v4.py`; local outputs use the
`lab-skeleton-regions-v4` prefix. Prior sources/exports are preserved.
Both fidelity tiers retain the same regions. Normal target: 32k triangles;
Ultra target: 190k. Region batching costs more draws than V3's single group.

Review candidate only: the current app still uses V3. Region selection,
highlighting, information cards and extraction are not yet wired into the UI.
This does not claim all 206 individual adult bones, tiny skull bones, or complete
joint surfaces. Review before replacing the approved skeleton in the app.
